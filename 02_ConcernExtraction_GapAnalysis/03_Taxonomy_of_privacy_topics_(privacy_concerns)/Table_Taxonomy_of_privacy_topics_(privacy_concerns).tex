\rowcolor{gray!30} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Privacy Topics -- Data Lifecycle}} \\* \addlinespace[3pt]
\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{A1. Data Collection}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
A1.1 Input Content  & Collection and handling of user inputs (including text, voice, images, and files). & \scriptsize "Does OpenAI store my prompts?", "Is my voice recorded?", "What happens to images I upload?" \\
A1.2 Behavioral Metadata & Collection of behavioral data and metadata (usage patterns, device info, IP, timestamps). & \scriptsize "Do they track when and how often I use it?", "Is my IP address logged?" \\

\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{A2. Data Usage}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
A2.1 Service Provision & Data usage for providing services (response generation, personalization, UX improvement). & \scriptsize "How is my data used to generate responses?", "Is my history used to personalize?" \\
A2.2 Model Training & Data usage for AI model training and fundamental improvements (opt-out, scope). & \scriptsize "Is my data used to train the model?", "Can I opt out of training?" \\

\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{A3. Data Retention \& Deletion}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
A3.1 Retention Duration & Data retention periods, storage locations, and lifecycle management. & \scriptsize "How long do they keep my data?", "Where is my data stored?" \\
A3.2 Deletion Mechanism & Processes for handling deletion requests, verification, and completeness. & \scriptsize "Can I delete my conversation history?", "Is deletion really permanent?" \\

\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{A4. Data Sharing}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
A4.1 Third Party Sharing & Data sharing with third parties (advertisers, business partners, government). & \scriptsize "Do they sell my data to advertisers?", "Can government access my chats?" \\
A4.2 Plugin Extension Access & Access permissions for plugins, custom GPTs, Actions, or extensions. & \scriptsize "Can Custom GPTs see my data?", "What data do plugins access?" \\

\midrule

\rowcolor{gray!30} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Privacy Topics -- User Rights \& Control}} \\* \addlinespace[3pt]
\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{B1. Privacy Awareness \& Control}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
B1.1 Consent Mechanism & Mechanisms for obtaining privacy consent (opt-in/out, defaults, scope). & \scriptsize "Did I consent to data collection?", "What privacy terms am I agreeing to?" \\
B1.2 Granular Control & Specific privacy control options (Memory toggle, training opt-out, history). & \scriptsize "Can I turn off memory?", "How do I opt out of training?" \\
B1.3 Transparency Disclosure & Clarity and disclosure of data processing flows, purposes, and practices. & \scriptsize "What exactly happens to my data?", "Why isn't this disclosed clearly?" \\
B1.4 Policy Change Notification & Notification and communication of privacy policy updates (alerts, explanations). & \scriptsize "Did they change the policy without telling me?", "How will I know if policy changes?" \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{gray!30} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Privacy Topics -- AI-Specific Risks}} \\* \addlinespace[3pt]
\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{C1. Model Behavior}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
C1.1 Output Risk & Privacy risks in AI outputs (data leakage, privacy inference, hallucinations). & \scriptsize "Can ChatGPT leak other users' data?", "What if AI generates my private info?" \\
C1.2 Memory Personalization & Privacy implications of memory functions (what is remembered, usage, clearance). & \scriptsize "What does Memory remember about me?", "Can I see what it remembers?" \\

\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{C2. Autonomous \& Emerging Applications}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
C2.1 Agent Autonomous Actions & Privacy boundaries for Agent autonomous actions (browsing, code execution). & \scriptsize "What can the AI agent access?", "Can it browse my files?", "What data does web browsing collect?" \\
C2.2 Downstream Integration & Privacy boundaries in downstream integration (API, Embodied AI, IoT, Third-party). & \scriptsize "What happens to my data when apps use ChatGPT API?", "Do smart devices share my conversations?" \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{gray!30} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Privacy Topics -- Compliance \& Protection}} \\* \addlinespace[3pt]
\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{D1. Regulatory Compliance}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
D1.1 Jurisdiction Law  & Applicable laws, cross-border data transfers, and regional privacy rights. & \scriptsize "Which laws apply to me?", "Is my data transferred to other countries?" \\
D1.2 Vulnerable Population & Privacy protection for specific groups (children, elderly, sensitive users). & \scriptsize "Is it safe for my child?", "What about COPPA compliance?" \\

\rowcolor{gray!10} \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{D2. Security \& Incidents}} \\* \addlinespace[2pt]
D2.1 Data Security & Security measures for privacy data (encryption, access control, secure storage). & \scriptsize "Is my data encrypted?", "How do they protect my conversations?" \\
D2.2 Breach Notification & Notification procedures and remedial measures for data privacy breaches. & \scriptsize "Will they tell me if there's a breach?", "What happens if my data is leaked?" \\
