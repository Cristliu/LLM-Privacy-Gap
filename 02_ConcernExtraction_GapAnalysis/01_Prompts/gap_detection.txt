## PRIVACY POLICY GAP TYPE TAXONOMY (7 Types in 2 Categories)

**⚠️ ALL  PRIVACY POLICY  gap types below concern PRIVACY and PERSONAL DATA. Only classify gaps that clearly relate to the PRIVACY policy deficiencies regarding PERSONAL DATA.**

### PRIVACY_POLICY_COVERAGE_GAPS (Objective Privacy Gaps - Policy Content Deficiencies)

These gaps represent **objective deficiencies** in PRIVACY policy content regarding **PERSONAL DATA** that can be verified by reading the policy text.
**CRITICAL**: If the user has a PRIVACY concern about PERSONAL DATA and the policy does NOT adequately cover it, and more importantly, based on comprehensive knowledge of legal provisions and relevant background, determine whether this concern should be addressed in the privacy policy and provide justification. If necessary, further assess what type of privacy gap it represents. → classify as PRIVACY_POLICY_COVERAGE_GAPS (G1-G4, and NO_GAP).

| Type | Code | Description | Specific PRIVACY Scope | When NOT to Use |
|------|------|-------------|----------------|------------------|
| **PRIVACY_POLICY_DETAIL_VAGUE** | G1 | PRIVACY policy **explicitly mentions** the topic AND uses **demonstrably vague language** that **FAILS to meet legal disclosure requirements, and it is necessary to appear in the Privacy Policy.** | **STRICT CRITERIA - ALL must be met**: (1) Topic is LEGALLY MANDATED to disclose (GDPR Art.13-14, CCPA); (2) Policy explicitly addresses the topic; (3) Language is OBJECTIVELY vague ("may", "reasonable", "appropriate" without ANY specifics); (4) Vagueness prevents user from understanding their rights | ❌ Policy simply doesn't mention a topic (may be NO_GAP); ❌ Topic is not legally required; ❌ Policy provides SOME specifics even if not perfect; ❌ User just wants MORE detail than legally required |
| **PRIVACY_AI_FEATURE_UNADDRESSED** | G2 | **AI-specific PRIVACY features** regarding **PERSONAL DATA** have NO policy coverage, and it is necessary to appear in the Privacy Policy. | **ONLY** for AI-specific PRIVACY capabilities: (1) Memory/Personalization PERSONAL DATA handling unclear; (2) Multi-modal PERSONAL DATA processing undocumented; (3) AI Agent/autonomous features PERSONAL DATA access not mentioned; (4) PERSONAL DATA usage in model training unclear | ❌ Do NOT use for general AI functionality questions |
| **PRIVACY_VULNERABLE_GROUP_NEGLECTED** | G3 | No specific PRIVACY provisions for vulnerable users' **PERSONAL DATA**, and it is necessary to appear in the Privacy Policy. | Missing children/minors PERSONAL DATA protections; No COPPA PERSONAL DATA compliance; No elderly user PERSONAL DATA considerations | ❌ Do NOT use unless concern explicitly involves minors/elderly |
| **PRIVACY_JURISDICTION_UNCLEAR** | G4 | Cross-border **PERSONAL DATA** handling not clearly addressed, and it is necessary to appear in the Privacy Policy. | Unclear PERSONAL DATA storage location; Missing international PERSONAL DATA transfer info; Unclear applicable PRIVACY laws for PERSONAL DATA (GDPR, CCPA) | ❌ Do NOT use unless concern explicitly involves cross-border/jurisdiction |
| **NO_GAP** | NO_GAP | NO GAP, or No need to include it in the Privacy Policy | Absolutely certain that a GAP exists |

**⚠️ CRITICAL: G1 HAS THE HIGHEST BAR - NOT A FALLBACK CATEGORY**

**G1 (PRIVACY_POLICY_DETAIL_VAGUE)** requires ALL of the following:
1. ✅ The topic is **LEGALLY MANDATED** to disclose (cite specific regulation: GDPR Art.X, CCPA §X)
2. ✅ The policy **EXPLICITLY MENTIONS** the topic (not just missing)
3. ✅ The language is **OBJECTIVELY VAGUE** - uses only hedge words without ANY concrete details
4. ✅ The vagueness **PREVENTS users from understanding their data rights**

**Examples of TRUE G1 (very strict):**
- Policy says "We retain data for a reasonable period" when GDPR Art.13(2)(a) REQUIRES specific retention periods → G1
- Policy says "We may share with partners" when CCPA REQUIRES listing categories of third parties → G1

**Examples of NOT G1 (common mistakes):**
- ❌ Policy doesn't mention a feature → This is NOT G1 (might be G2 or NO_GAP)
- ❌ Policy says "30 days for chat data, longer for account data" → This IS specific, NOT G1
- ❌ User wants more detail than policy provides → Personal preference is NOT G1
- ❌ Topic is not legally required to disclose → Cannot be G1

## TREE OF THOUGHTS (ToT) ANALYSIS METHODOLOGY for PRIVACY Gaps

**⚠️ REMINDER: All analysis must focus on PRIVACY and PERSONAL DATA. If the concern is not about PRIVACY OR no essential information to appear in the Privacy Policy, mark as invalid.**

### Phase 1: USER CONCERN UNDERSTANDING
**Objective**: Deeply understand the user's PRIVACY concern before examining the policy.

Analyze the provided concern information:
1. **Core Question**: What specific PRIVACY question is the user asking about their PERSONAL DATA?
2. **User Expectation**: What does the user expect regarding their PERSONAL DATA handling?
3. **Privacy Topic**: Which privacy_topic does this concern belong to?
4. **Answer Criteria**: What kind of policy content would adequately address this concern?
   - Does the user need specific timelines? (e.g., retention duration)
   - Does the user need specific mechanisms? (e.g., deletion process)
   - Does the user need specific scope? (e.g., what data is collected)
   - Does the user need specific controls? (e.g., opt-out options)

**Output of Phase 1**: A clear understanding of what answer we need to find in the Policy.

### Phase 2.0: NECESSITY ASSESSMENT (Necessity Assessment) - CRITICAL INTERNAL STEP
**Objective**: Before classifying gaps, determine whether this topic SHOULD be covered in a privacy policy.

**⚠️ This step prevents over-classification of gaps. Not every missing topic is a gap.**

**Consider the following (internal reasoning only - do not output separately):**

1. **Legal & Regulatory Requirements**:
   - Is disclosure MANDATED by GDPR (Art.13-14), CCPA, COPPA, or other regulations?
   - Would non-disclosure constitute a legal violation?

2. **Industry Standards**:
   - Do ALL major AI providers (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Microsoft) disclose this?
   - Is this a universally expected disclosure?

3. **Core Privacy Impact**:
   - Does this directly affect user's ability to make informed decisions about their PERSONAL DATA?
   - Is this a fundamental data practice (collection, use, retention, sharing, deletion)?

**Necessity Threshold**:
- **MUST DISCLOSE**: Legal mandate OR universal industry standard → Proceed to gap classification
- **SHOULD NOT EXPECT**: Niche feature, implementation detail, service quality issue → **NO_GAP**

**⚠️ If the concern is NOT something privacy policies are legally or universally expected to cover → classify as NO_GAP immediately.**


### Phase 2: POLICY REVIEW WITH CONCERN IN MIND (Read Policy with the provided concern in mind)
**Objective**: Read through {provider}'s Privacy Policy with the user's concern in mind, looking for answers.

#### Step 2.1: Holistic Policy Reading
Read the entire Privacy Policy to understand:
- Overall structure and main sections
- {provider}'s general approach to PRIVACY and PERSONAL DATA
- Key commitments and practices mentioned

#### Step 2.2: Targeted Analysis
With the user's specific concern in mind, evaluate:
1. **Does the Policy directly address this concern?**
   - Is there a section that specifically covers the user's question?
   - Does the content provide a clear, actionable answer?

2. **If content is found, assess its quality:**
   - Is the language specific or vague? (e.g., "30 days" vs "reasonable time")
   - Does it address the EXACT concern or just the general topic?
   - Are there clear processes, timelines, or mechanisms?

3. **If content is NOT found:**
   - Is this topic completely absent from the Policy?
   - Is it mentioned indirectly but not adequately covered?

#### Step 2.3: Extract Evidence**
- **If relevant content found**: Quote the EXACT policy text as evidence
- **If content is vague**: Quote the vague text to prove its vagueness
- **If content is missing**: Mark as "N/A" and note which sections were reviewed

#### Step 2.4: Determine Coverage Status**
- **ADEQUATE**: Policy provides clear, specific, actionable information
- **VAGUE**: Policy mentions the topic but lacks specific details
- **NOT_FOUND**: Policy does not address this topic at all


### Phase 3: GAP CLASSIFICATION via Tree of Thoughts
**Objective**: Based on Phase 2 findings, use Tree of Thoughts to systematically classify the gap.

### Final Output Determination
Based on the ToT analysis:
- **gap_detected**: true if ANY gap exists, false if NO_GAP
- **gap_category**: Primary category (PRIVACY_POLICY_COVERAGE_GAPS | PRIVACY_USER_PERCEPTION_GAPS | NO_GAP)
  - If BOTH categories apply, use PRIVACY_POLICY_COVERAGE_GAPS as primary
  - **⚠️ NO_GAP is a valid and expected outcome** - not every concern is a gap
- **gap_types**: List ONLY gap types that CLEARLY match (can be empty for NO_GAP)
- **coverage_status**: NOT_FOUND | VAGUE | ADEQUATE | NOT_APPLICABLE
